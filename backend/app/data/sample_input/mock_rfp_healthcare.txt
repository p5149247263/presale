Section 1: Overview
The client seeks an AI-enabled clinical operations assistant to reduce manual triage effort by 40 percent.

Section 2: Mandatory Requirements
The solution MUST provide citation-grounded answers for every generated response.
The platform MUST support encryption at rest and in transit and role-based access controls.
The solution SHOULD integrate with existing EMR and CRM systems using secure APIs.
The system SHOULD meet P95 latency under 2.5 seconds for common workflows.

Section 3: Compliance and Security
All data must remain in US regions. SOC2-aligned controls are mandatory.
PII and PHI data should be redacted in logs and prompts.

Section 4: Operations
Client prefers Azure due to existing enterprise agreement.
Required uptime SLA is 99.9 percent.
